# V1.22: Financial Telemetry, Audit, And Observability Governance

**Status:** Backlog
**Milestone:** M2
**Labels:** `epic`, `operations`, `audit`, `security`, `trust`, `platform`, `enhancement`

Turn telemetry from scattered runtime snapshots and future UI assumptions into
a first-class Tickoni product surface: privacy-preserving by default,
enterprise-exportable when configured, tamper-evident for financial review, and
useful for operators who need to prove why an AI-assisted financial output was
allowed, blocked, approved, or changed.

This epic is the missing owner for the large telemetry obligations currently
referenced by V1.12 hook telemetry, V1.14 link/process health, V1.19 health
panels, and `doc/execution/telemetry.md`. It does not replace those stories.
It defines the telemetry product, privacy, audit, export, retention, and
analytics foundation they should plug into.

## Product Intent

Generic agent tools can show that an agent called a model or tool. Tickoni must
prove something stronger for finance: which evidence supported a financial
answer, what data left the environment, which policy allowed or blocked the
request, whether human approval was required, who approved it, and whether the
final output changed after approval.

The product posture should combine:

- Hermes-style local-first privacy defaults: no outbound telemetry in local-only
  mode, local encrypted logs, and no raw prompt or response export by default.
- OpenClaw and Claude Code-style enterprise observability: OpenTelemetry export
  for metrics, traces, and logs to a customer-owned collector when enabled.
- Finance-grade audit controls: append-only, hash-chained, signed, retained,
  redacted-by-default, and replay-aware evidence for money-adjacent workflows.

Tickoni should not copy a generic agent analytics model blindly. Finance users
need operational visibility, cost control, and analytics, but they also handle
PII, account data, trading intent, portfolio holdings, restricted research,
client mandates, regulated communications, secrets, and prompt-injection risk.
Raw content capture must be opt-in, scoped, time-limited, admin-approved, and
audited.

## Users And Jobs

- Runtime owner: see whether tiles, links, queues, models, tools, adapters,
  policy, audit, replay, and export paths are healthy without scraping raw
  process state.
- Compliance or risk reviewer: prove which evidence, assumptions, market data,
  citations, policy decisions, approvals, and output hashes supported a
  financial answer or proposal.
- Security reviewer: verify that secrets, PII, trading-sensitive content, client
  data, prompt text, tool inputs, and model responses are redacted or retained
  only under explicit policy.
- Enterprise administrator: choose local-only, customer-owned observability, or
  managed Tickoni Cloud telemetry mode with region, retention, tenant isolation,
  BYOK/KMS, and content-capture controls.
- Finance team lead: understand AI use, model spend, error rates, stale-data
  rates, policy-intervention rates, approval rates, and client-delivery risk.
- CaseOps operator: distinguish policy denial from adapter degradation, stale
  data, queue backpressure, model budget exhaustion, replay divergence, or
  diagnostic failure.

## Success Metrics

- Tickoni has an explicit telemetry mode setting with local-only, enterprise
  observability, and managed cloud modes documented and enforced fail-closed.
- No raw prompts, model responses, tool inputs or outputs, secrets, account
  identifiers, portfolio data, or client data are exported by default.
- `tkmetr` and `tkdiag` own production metrics and diagnostics surfaces instead
  of leaving feature stories to invent their own telemetry path.
- Operational logs, cost/usage logs, security logs, and financial audit trails
  have separate schemas, retention policy, access policy, and evidence rules.
- Financial audit events are append-only, hash-chained, batch-signable, and
  replay-aware, with content hashes and metadata preserved when raw content is
  redacted.
- OpenTelemetry export supports customer-owned collectors with Tickoni financial
  semantic attributes and bounded, low-cardinality labels.
- Policy blocks, prompt-injection detections, data-exfiltration attempts,
  approval decisions, stale-data warnings, uncited outputs, and client-delivery
  markers are visible in metrics, diagnostics, audit, and CaseOps where
  applicable.
- Dashboards answer operational, security, cost, and finance-risk questions
  without requiring raw content capture.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: run a deterministic CaseOps or CLI demo in local-only mode,
  then rerun with enterprise OpenTelemetry export pointed at a local fixture
  collector.
- Expected result: local-only mode emits encrypted local logs, redacted
  financial audit JSONL, `tkmetr` metrics, and `tkdiag` diagnostics with no
  outbound telemetry; enterprise mode exports only configured redacted metrics,
  traces, and logs with Tickoni semantic attributes; raw content remains absent
  unless an admin-approved content-capture policy is explicitly enabled.

## Scope

### In Scope

- First-class telemetry modes: local-only, enterprise observability, and managed
  Tickoni Cloud mode.
- Redacted-by-default prompt, response, tool, adapter, model, approval, and
  financial-output telemetry policy.
- Separate operational logs, cost/usage logs, security logs, and financial audit
  trails.
- Production ownership model for `tkmetr` metrics and `tkdiag` diagnostics.
- OpenTelemetry export for metrics, traces, and logs to customer-owned
  collectors when enabled.
- Tickoni financial semantic attributes for use case, actor role, policy
  decision, approval state, data classification, model risk tier, output type,
  citation coverage, and evidence quality.
- Tamper-evident financial audit trail with append-only events, hash chaining,
  signed batches, WORM/object-lock export planning, retention policy, and legal
  hold planning.
- Finance-specific redaction and data classification for PII, account numbers,
  IBAN/routing/card data, secrets, portfolio holdings, trading intent,
  unreleased research, client mandates, and regulated communications.
- Prompt-injection, data-exfiltration, stale-data, uncited-output, and
  restricted-data telemetry and blocked-flow evidence.
- Human approval telemetry for risky actions and regulated outputs.
- Enterprise admin controls for telemetry policy, content capture, retention,
  region pinning, BYOK/KMS, SSO/SAML/OIDC, SCIM, RBAC/ABAC, SIEM export, and
  audit export API.
- Analytics dashboards for usage, spend, model quality, policy blocks,
  stale-data rate, uncited-claim rate, assumption-change rate,
  policy-intervention rate, human-override rate, and client-delivery exposure.

### Out Of Scope

- No direct live trading, payment, crypto, broker, TigerBeetle, or execution
  authority is added by telemetry.
- No raw prompt, response, tool I/O, account data, portfolio data, client data,
  or restricted research export by default.
- No generic third-party analytics SDK embedded in the deterministic financial
  event path.
- No duplicate metrics registry, tracing bootstrap, or background telemetry
  daemon outside the `tkmetr` and `tkdiag` ownership model.
- No high-cardinality metric labels such as event IDs, account IDs, case IDs,
  wallet addresses, raw prompts, raw errors, stack traces, or client names.
- No UI-only policy, approval, redaction, retention, or telemetry enforcement.
- No managed Tickoni Cloud data processing without explicit data-processing,
  regional storage, tenant isolation, retention, and customer-key decisions.

## Competitive And Regulatory Lessons

- Hermes demonstrates the value of local-first defaults, local logs, and no
  external analytics collection as the privacy baseline.
- OpenClaw demonstrates a useful split between local gateway logs and optional
  OpenTelemetry export when diagnostics and the export plugin are enabled.
- Claude Code demonstrates the need to separate vendor operational telemetry
  from customer-owned OpenTelemetry export, and to keep raw prompts, responses,
  tool parameters, and API bodies redacted unless explicitly enabled.
- OWASP GenAI guidance treats prompt injection and sensitive-information
  disclosure as first-order LLM application risks. Tickoni telemetry must
  expose attempted injection, unauthorized tool use, and data exfiltration
  without exporting sensitive content by default.
- EU AI Act-style lifecycle event logging expectations and financial-sector
  retention duties mean Tickoni should make retention configurable policy, not
  a hard-coded period.
- NIST AI RMF and financial-sector AI risk-management expectations imply that
  telemetry fields should map to governance, risk, monitoring, and evidence
  controls rather than generic agent activity only.

### V1.22.S1: Telemetry modes, privacy posture, and customer data boundaries

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** V1.22
**Parent roadmap item:** V1.22: Financial Telemetry, Audit, And Observability Governance
**Labels:** `story`, `security`, `enhancement`

**Description:** Define telemetry mode as a first-class Tickoni setting with
local-only, enterprise observability, and managed Tickoni Cloud modes.

#### Product Outcome

Administrators can choose how telemetry behaves before any operator trusts the
runtime, and local-only mode competes with privacy-first local agents without
weakening finance-grade audit.

#### User Story

As an enterprise administrator, I want explicit telemetry modes and data
boundaries, so that Tickoni never exports sensitive financial content unless my
organization has approved that exact behavior.

#### Scope

- In scope: local-only mode, enterprise observability mode, managed cloud mode,
  outbound-telemetry defaults, mode preflight, environment/config validation,
  and user-visible mode indicators.
- Out of scope: implementing managed cloud storage, live financial execution,
  or raw content capture without a separate admin-approved policy.

#### Preconditions And Assumptions

- Current Phase 0 telemetry is in-memory snapshots and supervisor output.
- `tkmetr` and `tkdiag` are planned Tickoni-owned telemetry surfaces.
- Telemetry configuration must fail closed when missing, malformed, or
  incompatible with the selected runtime tier.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given local-only mode, when Tickoni runs a deterministic demo, then no
      outbound telemetry is attempted and local telemetry locations are visible.
- [ ] Given enterprise observability mode, when no customer collector endpoint
      is configured, then telemetry export fails closed or remains disabled
      with an explicit diagnostic.
- [ ] Given managed cloud mode, when regional storage, tenant isolation,
      retention, data-processing, and customer-key decisions are absent, then
      the mode cannot be enabled.
- [ ] Given any mode, when raw content capture is not explicitly enabled, then
      prompts, responses, tool I/O, account data, portfolio data, and client
      data are not exported.

#### Conditional Acceptance

**Financial capability and policy**

- [ ] N/A - this story defines telemetry modes, not capability policy.

**Audit and replay**

- [ ] Telemetry mode and content-capture policy are captured in audit/replay
      metadata where they affect evidence interpretation.

**Runtime topology and tile ownership**

- [ ] Mode selection does not add a new telemetry daemon outside `tkmetr` and
      `tkdiag`.

**Model, tool, adapter, or execution boundary**

- [ ] Mode selection does not grant direct model, tool, adapter, or execution
      access.

**CaseOps API or UI**

- [ ] CaseOps and CLI surfaces can display the effective telemetry mode and
      content-capture state.

### V1.22.S2: Redaction, data classification, and content-capture controls

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** V1.22
**Parent roadmap item:** V1.22: Financial Telemetry, Audit, And Observability Governance
**Labels:** `story`, `security`, `enhancement`

**Description:** Make finance-specific redaction and data classification the
default for logs, telemetry, audit metadata, and optional content capture.

#### Product Outcome

Tickoni can preserve proof through hashes and metadata while preventing
sensitive financial content from leaking into logs, metrics, traces, or
analytics by default.

#### User Story

As a security reviewer, I want sensitive content redacted and classified before
it reaches telemetry, so that logs and exports are useful without becoming a
secondary data breach surface.

#### Scope

- In scope: redaction categories for PII, financial identifiers, secrets,
  trading-sensitive content, restricted research, client mandates, and custom
  institution dictionaries or regexes.
- Out of scope: using redaction as a substitute for policy checks, audit hash
  chaining, or provider egress controls.

#### Preconditions And Assumptions

- Redaction must preserve content hashes and structured metadata where possible
  so auditors can prove involvement without exposing raw content.
- High-cardinality values remain forbidden as metric labels.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given sensitive content in a prompt, tool input, adapter response, or
      financial output, when telemetry is emitted, then raw content is redacted
      and content hashes or safe metadata are retained where configured.
- [ ] Given custom institution redaction rules, when content is classified,
      then those rules apply before export or local log persistence.
- [ ] Given explicit content-capture approval, when raw content is retained,
      then scope, duration, actor, approval reason, and retention policy are
      logged.

#### Conditional Acceptance

**Financial capability and policy**

- [ ] Data classification can inform policy decisions but does not replace
      `tkpoly` capability checks.

**Audit and replay**

- [ ] Redacted audit records preserve enough metadata and hashes for replay and
      evidence comparison without requiring raw sensitive content by default.

**Runtime topology and tile ownership**

- [ ] N/A - this story does not change tile IDs or links.

**Model, tool, adapter, or execution boundary**

- [ ] Model, tool, and adapter telemetry applies redaction before export.

**CaseOps API or UI**

- [ ] CaseOps displays redaction and content-capture status without revealing
      redacted content to unauthorized users.

### V1.22.S4: Tamper-evident financial audit trail and retention policy

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** V1.22
**Parent roadmap item:** V1.22: Financial Telemetry, Audit, And Observability Governance
**Labels:** `story`, `audit`, `enhancement`

**Description:** Define the append-only financial audit ledger for AI-assisted
outputs, signed log batches, immutable export, retention, and legal hold.

#### Product Outcome

Compliance and risk reviewers can prove which financial answer or proposal was
shown, which evidence supported it, which policy allowed it, and whether the
audit trail was tampered with.

#### User Story

As a compliance reviewer, I want every AI-assisted financial output to have a
tamper-evident audit trail, so that post-event review can prove what happened
without trusting mutable logs.

#### Scope

- In scope: append-only audit events, hash chaining, batch signatures, WORM or
  object-lock export planning, retention policy, legal hold, UTC timestamp,
  actor, session, model, tool, data source, approval, output hash, and replay
  linkage.
- Out of scope: making metrics the audit source of truth, storing mutable audit
  summaries as authoritative, or enabling privileged execution.

#### Preconditions And Assumptions

- Existing Tickoni audit invariants require append-only, hash-chained records.
- Replay must run with external effects disabled and substitute captured
  outputs.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given an AI-assisted financial output, when it is finalized or proposed,
      then the audit trail includes model version, data sources, market-data
      snapshot, calculation inputs, retrieved documents, approvals, and output

- [ ] Given a tampered audit event or missing link, when the chain is verified,

- [ ] Given legal hold or retention policy, when export or deletion is
      requested, then policy controls whether data is retained, redacted,
      exported, or blocked.
- [ ] Given replay, when audit evidence is inspected, then no live model,

### V1.22.S5: Financial semantic attributes, cost usage, and model governance signals

**Status:** Backlog

**Description:** Define Tickoni-specific telemetry attributes and usage/cost

#### Product Outcome
