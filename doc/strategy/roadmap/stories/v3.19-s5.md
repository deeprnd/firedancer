# V3.19.S5: Proof Black Box (PROOF Function)

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** #[github-epic-issue]
**Parent roadmap item:** V3.19: Tickoni Native Investment Terminal
**Labels:** `type/story`, `area/audit`

Render the trust layer in the PROOF function panel: event hash, policy hash,
proposal hash, model route, adapter attribution, actor, agent, approval state,
replay status, tamper check, timeline, proof export, and divergence details.

## Product Outcome

Compliance or risk reviewers can verify what happened, why it was allowed or
blocked, which evidence was used, and whether replay matched without reading
raw audit JSONL or replay logs — all within the PROOF function panel.

## User Story

As a compliance or risk reviewer, I want a black-box view of the investment
decision, so that I can verify provenance, replay status, and tamper evidence
from the terminal.

## Scope

- In scope: audit timeline with 9-step sequence (event received, intent
  normalized, basket built, rejected instruments recorded, ticket validated,
  policy decision, proposal signed, paper fill ready, replay capsule sealed);
  hash fields (event hash, policy hash, proposal hash); evidence references;
  model route (e.g., offline/mock); adapter attribution (e.g., paper_broker:v0);
  actor and agent identity; approval state; replay status (MATCH, DIVERGE,
  MISSING); tamper check (CLEAN, TAMPERED); proof bundle export affordance
  (disabled or fixture-only until governed export endpoint exists); divergence
  details (first differing field and sequence when present); `/show proof` or
  `/show audit` command activating the PROOF function from another panel.
- Out of scope: changing audit record schema, replay substitution behavior,
  proof hash algorithms, or external auditor portal access.

## Preconditions And Assumptions

- V1.18-style evidence and replay proof data is available as fixtures or
  `tk_api` read responses.
- Export affordance can be disabled or fixture-only until a governed export
  endpoint exists.
- The PROOF function panel renders inside the terminal shell from S1.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a replay-match fixture, when the PROOF function renders, then
      event hash, policy hash, proposal hash, evidence refs, approval state,
      replay status, and timeline are visible.
- [ ] Given a tamper fixture, when the PROOF function renders, then replay
      status shows divergence with first differing field and sequence when
      available.
- [ ] Given a missing or stale replay capsule fixture, when the PROOF function
      renders, then proof export is disabled and the reason is visible.
- [ ] Given the `/show proof` or `/show audit` command, when submitted, then
      the PROOF function is activated and scrolls into focus from another panel.

### Conditional Acceptance

**Financial capability and policy**

- [ ] Policy version and outcome are visible near the proof summary.

**Audit and replay**

- [ ] Hashes, evidence refs, replay capsule id, replay status, and divergence
      details are loaded from runtime or fixture data without recomputation in
      UI display code.

**Runtime topology and tile ownership**

- [ ] N/A - no topology changes.

**Model, tool, adapter, or execution boundary**

- [ ] Model route and adapter attribution are display-only and cannot trigger
      calls.

**CaseOps API or UI**

- [ ] Black-box data is loadable from a deterministic fixture or documented
      `tk_api` read response.

**Storage role: Memory, Analytics, Ledger**

- [ ] N/A - proof data is sourced from `tk_api` or fixtures; the UI does
      not connect to audit files or replay files directly.

**Metrics, diagnostics, and operations**

- [ ] N/A - no new metrics or diagnostic signals are introduced.

**Security and fail-closed behavior**

- [ ] Given unavailable or malformed proof data, when the PROOF function
      renders, then the panel shows unavailable or evidence-required rather than
      inferred values.

## Evidence Plan

- Tests: render replay match, divergence, missing capsule, and disabled export.

  status.
- Blocked-flow evidence: stale or missing replay capsule fixture.
