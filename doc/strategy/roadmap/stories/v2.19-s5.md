# V2.19.S5: Audit, Replay, And Proof Black Box

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** #V2.19
**Parent roadmap item:** V2.19: Tickoni Terminal CaseOps UI
**Labels:** `story`, `audit`, `enhancement`

Render the trust layer: event hash, policy hash, proposal hash, model route,
adapter attribution, actor, agent, approval state, replay status, tamper check,
timeline, proof export, and divergence details.

## Product Outcome

Reviewers can verify what happened, why it was allowed or blocked, which
evidence was used, and whether replay matched without reading raw audit JSONL
or replay logs.

## User Story

As a compliance or partner reviewer, I want a black-box view of the money
decision, so that I can verify provenance, replay status, and tamper evidence
from the UI.

## Scope

- In scope: audit timeline with 9-step sequence (event received, intent
  normalized, basket built, rejected instruments recorded, ticket validated,
  policy decision, proposal signed, paper fill ready, replay capsule sealed),
  hash fields (event hash, policy hash, proposal hash), evidence references,
  model route (e.g., offline/mock), adapter attribution (e.g., paper_broker:v0),
  actor and agent identity, approval state, replay status (MATCH, DIVERGE,
  MISSING), tamper check (CLEAN, TAMPERED), proof bundle export affordance
  (disabled or fixture-only until governed export endpoint exists), and
  divergence details (first differing field and sequence when present).
- Out of scope: changing audit record schema, replay substitution behavior,
  proof hash algorithms, or external auditor portal access.

## Preconditions And Assumptions

- V1.18-style evidence and replay proof data is available as fixtures or
  `tkapi` read responses.
- Export affordance can be disabled or fixture-only until a governed export
  endpoint exists.
- The audit/replay panel renders inside the terminal shell from S1.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a replay-match fixture, when the black-box panel renders, then
      event hash, policy hash, proposal hash, evidence refs, approval state,
      replay status, and timeline are visible.
- [ ] Given a tamper fixture, when the panel renders, then replay status shows
      divergence with first differing field and sequence when available.
- [ ] Given a missing or stale replay capsule fixture, when the panel renders,
      then proof export is disabled and the reason is visible.
- [ ] Given the `/show audit` command, when submitted, then the audit panel
      scrolls into focus or is activated from another panel.

### Conditional Acceptance

**Financial capability and policy**

- [ ] Policy version and outcome are visible near the proof summary.

**Audit and replay**

- [ ] Hashes, evidence refs, replay capsule id, replay status, and divergence
      details are loaded from runtime or fixture data without recomputation in
      UI display code.

**Runtime topology and tile ownership**

- [ ] N/A - no topology changes.

**Model, tool, adapter, or execution boundary**

- [ ] Model route and adapter attribution are display-only and cannot trigger
      calls.

**CaseOps API or UI**

- [ ] Black-box data is loadable from a deterministic fixture or documented
      `tkapi` read response.

## Evidence Plan

- Demo or command: local UI flow using replay-match, tamper, and stale-capsule
  fixtures.
- Tests: render replay match, divergence, missing capsule, and disabled export.
- Fixtures or samples: clean proof bundle summary, tamper divergence summary,
  missing capsule summary.
- Audit/replay evidence: fixture fields for hashes, evidence refs, and replay
  status.
- Blocked-flow evidence: stale or missing replay capsule fixture.
