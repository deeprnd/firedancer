# V2.19.S2: Investment Safe-Action Workbench

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** #V2.19
**Parent roadmap item:** V2.19: Tickoni Terminal CaseOps UI
**Labels:** `story`, `investing`, `enhancement`

Render the V1.1/V1.11 investment safe-action flow: thesis input, normalized
intent, proposed basket, rejected candidates, ticket, policy gate, paper state,
and replay proof.

## Product Outcome

Users and operators can see how an investment thesis became a bounded proposal,
which instruments were rejected, which checks passed, and why the final action
is allowed, blocked, paper-only, or approval-required.

## User Story

As a consumer-money user, I want to inspect the policy-checked investment
proposal before any execution path, so that I understand the allowed
consequence and the denied alternatives.

## Scope

- In scope: investment intent input, normalized intent fields (amount, market,
  venues, asset classes, exclusions, concentration), basket table with ticker,
  role, allocation, price, and status, rejected candidates table with denial
  reason, trade ticket preview (order type, quantity), policy decision panel
  showing individual check results (cash check, buying power, per-order
  notional, daily notional, market/venue scope, restricted instrument,
  same-day round trip, minimum holding period), proposal hash, paper state
  indicator, and the `/explain denial` and `/show policy` commands.
- Out of scope: new basket construction logic, new trading policy rules, live
  brokerage placement, direct market-data calls from the UI, or new financial
  capability definitions.

## Preconditions And Assumptions

- V1.1/V1.11 investment fixture or `tkapi` mock data exists for the USD 2,000
  AI-infrastructure scenario.
- Restricted `SOXL` and oversize USD 25,000 flows are represented as denied
  or blocked fixture states.
- Paper placement state, when shown, is produced by the governed runtime path.
- The workbench renders inside the terminal shell from S1.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given the USD 2,000 investment fixture, when the workbench renders, then
      the normalized intent, allowed basket, ticket preview, and policy checks
      are visible in one workspace.
- [ ] Given a restricted instrument fixture, when the workbench renders, then
      the candidate appears in the rejected table with the exact denied reason
      and no quote or paper-fill affordance.
- [ ] Given an oversize notional fixture, when the workbench renders, then the
      policy gate shows the effective limit, decision, and disabled direct
      action controls.
- [ ] Given the `/explain denial` command, when submitted, then the denied
      candidate's reason (e.g., "restricted leveraged ETF", "asset class
      violation") is displayed prominently.

### Conditional Acceptance

**Financial capability and policy**

- [ ] Policy outcomes and scope are displayed from `tkpoly` output; UI code
      does not recompute allow or deny.

**Audit and replay**

- [ ] Proposal hash, policy hash, and replay status link to the audit/replay
      panel when present.

**Runtime topology and tile ownership**

- [ ] N/A - no topology changes.

**Model, tool, adapter, or execution boundary**

- [ ] Market, model, and adapter attribution is display-only and sourced from
      governed records.

**CaseOps API or UI**

- [ ] Workbench data is loadable from a deterministic fixture or documented
      `tkapi` read response.

- Audit/replay evidence: proposal hash and replay match display fixture.
- Blocked-flow evidence: restricted instrument and oversize notional fixtures.
