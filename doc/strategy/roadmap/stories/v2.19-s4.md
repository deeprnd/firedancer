# V2.19.S4: Portfolio And Cash Impact View

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** #V2.19
**Parent roadmap item:** V2.19: Tickoni Terminal CaseOps UI
**Labels:** `story`, `investing`, `enhancement`

Show before/after cash, buying power, exposure, concentration, pending
obligations, thesis health, approval state, and alert context for a proposed
money action.

## Product Outcome

Users and operators can understand consequence before action: what cash,
exposure, obligations, concentration, and approval state look like before and
after the proposal.

## User Story

As a consumer-money user, I want to see the projected impact of a proposed
trade or payment, so that I can decide whether the safe action still fits my
mandate and cash constraints.

## Scope

- In scope: before/after cash balance, before/after buying power, sector
  exposure (e.g., technology exposure percentage change), ETF exposure
  percentage, single-name concentration vs. limit, pending obligations
  (e.g., unexecuted payout awaiting approval), thesis health indicator,
  approval expiry countdown, and alert messages (e.g., "approval expires in
  4h", "cash buffer remains above target", "rebalance not required").
- Out of scope: portfolio optimization, rebalance automation, live account
  aggregation, authoritative balance storage, or new financial limits.

## Preconditions And Assumptions

- Impact data comes from governed fixture or `tkapi` response fields.
- TigerBeetle, DuckDB, and Markdown roles remain distinct; the UI does not
  connect directly to any store.
- The view renders inside the terminal shell from S1.
- Before/after values are snapshot-based, not live-streaming.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given the investment impact fixture, when the view renders, then cash,
      buying power, sector exposure, ETF exposure, single-name concentration,
      and pending obligations are shown before and after the proposal.
- [ ] Given a combined investment plus pending payout fixture, when the view
      renders, then the cash buffer and obligation alerts are visible.
- [ ] Given unavailable impact data, when the view renders, then consequence
      panels show unavailable or evidence-required rather than inferred values.
- [ ] Given the `/show impact` command, when submitted, then the impact view
      scrolls into focus or is activated from another panel.

### Conditional Acceptance

- [ ] Impact panels display account, notional, exposure, and approval state

**Audit and replay**

- [ ] Impact snapshot identifiers or evidence refs are shown when available.

**Runtime topology and tile ownership**

- [ ] N/A - no topology changes.

- [ ] N/A - no model, tool, adapter, or execution calls are introduced.
